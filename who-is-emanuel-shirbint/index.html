About the Researcher

Emanuel Shirbint

Emanuel Shirbint is a Data & AI Architect and independent researcher working at the intersection of governed agent systems, LLM safety and evaluation, BI/DWH architecture, clinical AI, human and medical digital twins, and institutional governance.

Research profile

Emanuel Shirbint is a Data & AI Architect and independent researcher. His work joins two threads: applied enterprise data architecture (BI/DWH, data engineering, data governance) and conceptual research on how AI systems can be made accountable — through governed agent architectures, structure-sensitive safety evaluation, governed abductive reasoning for medical and human digital twins, and the normative architecture that fixes what an AI system is for.

Professional background

His applied practice spans business-intelligence and data-warehouse architecture, data engineering, and the lineage, contracts and quality controls that trustworthy AI depends upon. This work provides the auditable data foundation referenced across his research programmes.

Clinical research experience

Emanuel Shirbint is a co-author on peer-reviewed clinical research conducted with the Multiple Sclerosis Center at Sheba Medical Center and collaborating institutions, including studies of COVID-19 vaccination safety and humoral response in multiple sclerosis, brain-volume mapping in primary-progressive MS, robust spine segmentation in MRI, and digital assessment of finger dexterity. These works are presented here historically and accurately, with each author position and the peer-review status stated on the record.

Data and AI architecture

Alongside clinical and conceptual research, his practice concerns the data architecture beneath accountable AI: warehouse and BI design, data contracts, lineage and quality controls that allow AI-assisted output to be audited rather than merely produced.
