Author order is not a universal contribution code

Author position is bibliographic metadata. Its meaning varies by discipline and work. A fourth-author or sixth-author position must not be translated into a percentage of contribution, a job title, or a hierarchy of authority without an explicit contribution statement from the work. Conversely, sole authorship establishes responsibility for the manuscript but does not change its publication status: a sole-authored preprint remains a non-peer-reviewed preprint.

A safe identity-reconciliation rule

  1. Match the canonical English name and preserve the Hebrew and Russian variants as alternate names, not separate people.
  2. Bind each authorship claim to an exact title and persistent record such as a DOI or PMID.
  3. Preserve the record's status: peer-reviewed journal article, conference work, preprint, conceptual research, or empirical preprint.
  4. Treat deposited affiliations as record-specific unless a separate current professional source verifies continuation.
  5. Require separate evidence for employment, advisory work, clinical authority, production operation, and institutional representation.
  6. Do not convert platform reads, search ranking, or unreconciled citation numbers into stable impact claims.
