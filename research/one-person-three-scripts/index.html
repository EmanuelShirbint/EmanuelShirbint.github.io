A five-link identity chain

  1. Name: record the exact spelling and script shown by the source.
  2. Work: match a title or other bounded object rather than relying on the name alone.
  3. Identifier: prefer DOI, PMID, library control number, ORCID, or another source-owned identifier.
  4. Context: compare co-authors, date, work-specific affiliation, subject area, and version lineage.
  5. Claim class: keep authorship, publication status, employment, advisory work, software, production systems, and impact separate.
