Evidence summary

The Version 2 public record reports a bounded seven-cycle, scaffolding-level self-modification experiment with a fixed foundation model and separate human promotion authority. The trace contains four promoted successor states, one failed non-promoted transition preserved in history, and two consecutive NO_CHANGE outcomes. The supported claim is limited to the tested local governance architecture; the record does not demonstrate artificial general intelligence, unbounded or exponential improvement, autonomous promotion, permanent saturation, or external production validation.
