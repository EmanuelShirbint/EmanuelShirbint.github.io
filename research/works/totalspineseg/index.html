TotalSpineSeg: Robust Spine Segmentation with Landmark-Based Labeling in MRI

Yehuda Warszawer, Nathan Molinier, Jan Valosek, Pierre-Louis Benveniste, Sandrine Bedard, Emanuel Shirbint, Feroze B. Mohamed, Charidimos Tsagkas, Shannon Kolind, Larry Lynd, Jiwon Oh, Alexandre Prat, Roger Tam, Anthony Traboulsee, Scott Patten, Lisa Eunyoung Lee, Anat Achiron, Julien Cohen-Adad

Emanuel Shirbint author position: 6 of 18

Affiliations: Multiple Sclerosis Center, Sheba Medical Center, Ramat Gan, Israel

Published / posted: 2025-03-16 · Official record · DOI

Abstract

Accurate segmentation and labeling of spinal structures are essential for diagnosing and managing spinal pathologies. TotalSpineSeg uses cascaded nnU-Net models and landmark-guided iterative labeling to segment vertebrae, intervertebral discs, spinal cord, and spinal canal across MRI contrasts.
