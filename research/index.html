    Emanuel Shirbint

    2026 · Preprints.org · doi:10.20944/preprints202608.0975.v2 · Version 2 · Not peer-reviewed

    A bounded seven-cycle scaffolding-level experiment with a fixed foundation model and separate human promotion authority. The trace reports four promoted states, one retained failed transition, and two NO_CHANGE outcomes; it does not establish AGI, unbounded improvement, autonomous promotion, permanent saturation, or external production validation.
