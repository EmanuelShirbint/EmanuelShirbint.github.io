Why the distinction matters

Discoverability, validation, and impact are different evidence dimensions. A registry can improve discovery without changing the underlying review status. A versioned DOI can preserve lineage without increasing the unique-work count. A citation number can vary across databases without supplying a reconciled public claim. Keeping these dimensions separate prevents ordinary metadata propagation from being overstated as scientific validation.
