Projects & Platforms

Applied and conceptual platforms connected to the research programmes. Concept-stage systems are labelled as such; no operational or clinical deployment is claimed.

MS-AGIP — Governed Medical Digital Twin (concept)

A governed abductive architecture for medical Patient Digital Twins in multiple sclerosis care, organised around seven runtime contours separating an ontology-guided Research Framework, a clinician-facing Neurologist Digital Twin and a patient-controlled Patient Digital Twin. Described in the MS-AGIP preprint; this is a conceptual framework, not a deployed clinical system.

LLM-Agent safety evaluation framework (concept & pilot)

Pressure-indexed, structure-sensitive evaluation of LLM agents, with the Masking Index diagnostic and held-out release gates. See Measuring What Survives and the topological robustness framework. Reported experiments are synthetic controlled pilots.

BI/DWH & data architecture practice

Applied business-intelligence and data-warehouse architecture and data engineering — the accountable data foundation beneath the research programmes. See the BI/DWH and Data Architecture area.
