Moral Grounding and Institutional AI Governance

A single propagation account connecting moral grounding, authorised volition, teleological translation, representation and AI execution.

Research questions

  • What upstream normative and volitional architecture fixes what an AI system is for?
  • How does local correctness propagate into global, systemic error?
  • What does it mean that AI executes an operational representation of authorised will rather than legitimising ends itself?
