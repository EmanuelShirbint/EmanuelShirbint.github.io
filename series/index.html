Research Series

Research Series & Programmes

Related works are organised into programmes. Each series page distinguishes published, preprint, under-review and planned work explicitly.

Governed Medical Digital Twins / MS-AGIP

A governed abductive architecture for medical Patient Digital Twins, operationalised for multiple sclerosis care in the MS-AGIP platform.

1 constituent work(s) · One conceptual-framework preprint (not peer-reviewed). Validation is proposed, not yet performed.

Human Digital Twins and Self-Knowledge

Extending the governed abductive architecture from clinical modelling to human self-modelling and personality under pressure.

1 constituent work(s) · One conceptual-framework preprint (not peer-reviewed), with an earlier-titled revision in its version lineage.

LLM-Agent Safety and Topological Robustness

Structure-sensitive evaluation of LLM agents under engineered adversarial pressure, using pressure-indexed persistent homology and a masking diagnostic.

2 constituent work(s) · Two preprints (not peer-reviewed). Reported experiments are synthetic instrument-validation pilots; external protocols on public benchmarks are prospectively specified.

Moral Grounding and Institutional AI Governance

A single propagation account connecting moral grounding, authorised volition, teleological translation, representation and AI execution.

1 constituent work(s) · One conceptual-framework preprint (not peer-reviewed).

Clinical AI and Multiple Sclerosis Research

Peer-reviewed clinical research and applied imaging/data methods in multiple sclerosis, conducted with the Multiple Sclerosis Center at Sheba Medical Center and collaborators.

5 constituent work(s) · Four peer-reviewed journal articles and one preprint with associated code and conference material.

BI/DWH and Data Architecture

Applied business-intelligence, data-warehouse and data-engineering practice — the accountable data foundation beneath the research programmes.

0 constituent work(s) · Professional and applied practice. No standalone research manuscript in this archive; this programme provides the data-architecture foundation referenced across the other series.
